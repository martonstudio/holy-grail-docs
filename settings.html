Settings

CC and channel settings

The setup page shows the eight boxes: parts 1 to 4 (CH 9 to CH 12) and FX 5 to FX 8 (LOOPER, SPACE, DRIVE, HEAT). Each box shows its channel, its engine or effect and its CC numbers. The top strip has SETUP (opens AUDIO), SEND LOG and PROJECT.

  1. Tap a box.
  2. On its first page, set CHANNEL (1 to 16). On a part, set ENGINE here too.
  3. Press NEXT. Each slot has its own page: PART 1 SLOT 1 to SLOT 8, or FX n SLOT 1 to SLOT 8.

A slot page:

  • CONTROL: the control it moves.
  • ENABLED: tap to switch the row on or off. Patterns keep each row's on and off.
  • CC NUMBER: the number it answers to.
  • RANGE: drag the ends to limit what the CC covers.
  • CLEAR SLOT: removes the control.

A slot with no control says to long press a control in the editor to attach it.

A row cannot be enabled without a control. Two rows on one part cannot share a CC number.

COPY and PASTE (bottom right of the CH 16 box) copy a pattern. PASTE asks SURE?.

On the CH 16 box (HEAT), the slot pages also have MIXER LEVEL. It assigns a CC to a mixer level: PART 1 to 4, DIGITAKT or MASTER.

SEND LOG sends the app's log.

Routing settings

Tap SETUP on the setup page. The AUDIO sheet has four pages. Turn them with PREV and NEXT.

Page 1: AUDIO

  • BUFFER: 64, 128, 256 or 512 frames. Default 128. [TODO: when to change it]
  • ROUTING: A or B. Default B. See Setup.
  • MIC: OFF or ON. Default OFF.
  • OTHER APPS: SILENCE or MIX. [TODO: what it does]
  • INPUT: RAW or PROCESSED. [TODO: what it does]
  • INPUT GAIN: in dB, in half-dB steps.

Page 2: AUDIO status

RUNNING (with sample rate and buffer), the Digitakt setting for the chosen routing, INPUT, OUTPUT, FORMAT, LATENCY, INPUT PEAK. An audio start error shows here.

Page 3: DIGITAKT TRACKS

  • PATTERN: the channel whose Program Change selects a pattern. Default 16. Match it to the Digitakt's PROG CHG OUT channel.
  • T1 to T6: the Digitakt's track channels. The app sends CC 95 on them in routing B. Default 1 to 6.

Page 4: TRACKS

  • T7 and T8: the Digitakt's track channels. Default 7 and 8.
  • DEMO SONG: STOP or PLAY. Plays a built-in four-part demo. [TODO: what the demo song is for]
  • OUTPUT: DIGITAKT or PHONE. See Setup.

RECORD DT is on the recorder page. Routing: see Recorders.
