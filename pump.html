7 PUMP

Sidechain-style ducking, in time with your Digitakt.

PUMP lives on the mixer. The PUMP box draws the duck's shape. The row of 16 steps below it sets where it ducks. Each synth and the looper has its own PUMP amount in its mixer box.

FX PUMP sets whether the SPACE sends pump along with the synths.
