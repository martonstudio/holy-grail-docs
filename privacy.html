Holy Grail docs

Privacy policy

Holy Grail for iOS and Android. Effective 13 November 2026.

In short

Holy Grail has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you make stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

On your device

Projects, sounds, samples and recordings are saved in the app's own storage on your device. The microphone is used only to record audio you ask the app to record, and the camera only to record video you ask it to record. Recordings are saved on your device and are not uploaded.

The app talks to your controller over USB (MIDI and audio). That connection is local and nothing from it leaves the device.

Network

When the app opens it downloads a short message file from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, GitHub receives your device's IP address; the app sends nothing else with it. GitHub's handling of that request is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links in the app to these docs or to Discord open in your browser and fall under those sites' own policies.

Sharing

When you use SHARE, the app hands the file to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app sends nothing on its own.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: m@marton.studio.
