6 Samples

Every sampler machine loads through one browser. BROWSE on the main page opens it. It has two panes side by side, and each one shows HOLY_DISK, PHONE, RECORDER or DIGITAKT.

HOLY_DISK is the app's own disk, shared by every project. Keep your samples and sample packs there. COPY, MOVE and DELETE work from one pane to the other.

LOAD opens a folder or a zip file, and previews a sample. LOAD again puts it into the sampler.

AirDrop or share a sample or a folder to your iPhone and pick Open in Holy Grail. PHONE remembers the folders you pick in Files. IMMORTAL WAVES, a read-only library, comes with the app. DIGITAKT reads the Digitakt's samples while its sequencer is stopped.

A sound keeps its own copy of its samples, so moving or deleting files never breaks a saved sound.
