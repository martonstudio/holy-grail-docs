4 Sounds

Each project has 127 sound slots. Any slot holds a sound from any engine.

Load a sound by hand, or send a Program Change from the Digitakt. The slots are in two columns, eight to a page. Tap a name to load that slot. Tap its number to save the sound you are playing into it; a slot that already holds a sound asks SURE? first.
