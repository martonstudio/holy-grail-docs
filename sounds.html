4 Sounds

A project has one bank of 127 sound slots, shared by all four parts. A Program Change from the Digitakt loads a slot (0 loads slot 1). You can also load one in the app.

Sounds

On a synth page the bottom bar has the sound name, SAVE and BROWSE.

  1. Tap the sound name to open the SOUNDS list.
  2. Tap a row to load it. The buttons at the top jump between banks of slots. PREV and NEXT turn pages.
  3. SAVE writes into a row. On a filled row it asks SURE? first.

Tap the sound's name under the title to rename it. A dot beside the name means it has changed since it was loaded.

Browse

BROWSE opens presets for the part's engine: factory banks and your own (USER). They are shared by all projects. Tap a preset to load it. SEARCH filters by name. SAVE AS PRESET keeps the current sound.
