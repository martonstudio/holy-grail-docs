The main page

The main page shows the app's eight boxes. Tap a box to open it. Hold a box to switch it off or on.

BoxWhat it is
Top row, 1 to 4The four parts, on channels 9 to 12. Each runs one synth or sampler. The header shows the channel and the sound number, and the name under the picture is the sound. See Parts and engines.
LOOPThe looper, on channel 13. See Looper.
DRIVEDistortion, on channel 14. See DRIVE.
SPACEDelay and reverb, on channel 15. See SPACE.
MIXERThe mixer, on channel 16, with the playing pattern under it. See Mixer.
