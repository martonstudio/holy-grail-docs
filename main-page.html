2 The main page

Use your Digitakt as you always do. The app plays along.

The top row is your four synths. Tap one to open it. Hold it to mute it.

The bottom row is the looper and the three effects. Tap one to open it. Hold it to bypass it.

There are four main pages: this one, setup, mixer and recorder. PREV and NEXT move between them.
