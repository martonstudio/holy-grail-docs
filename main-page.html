2 The main page

Use your Digitakt as you always do. The app plays along.

The top row is your four synths. Each tile shows the number of the sound it is playing above its name. Tap a tile to open the synth. Hold it to mute it.

The bottom row is the looper, SPACE and DRIVE. Tap one to open it. Hold it to bypass it. The last box, MIXER, opens the mixer.

Every page has the navigation column at the right. PREV and NEXT turn to the neighbouring page, and UP goes one level up. A button is greyed where there is nowhere to go. There are three main pages: this one, setup and recorder.

Play a synth from a Digitakt MIDI track on its channel. Program Change loads a sound.

The playing pattern shows in the bottom right corner. It stays in sync with your Elektron sequencer when transport and program change are on in the box's MIDI settings.
