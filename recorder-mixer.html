7 Recorder and mixer

Mixer

The third main page. Six strips: the four parts, the Digitakt and master, each with a level, meter and CLIP light. A CC can move a level through MIXER LEVEL on the HEAT box.

Recorder

The fourth main page.

Audio: NEW, REC, STOP. VIDEO records the back camera with the app's audio. Tap a take in the FILES list to play it back. TRIM and DELETE ask SURE?. The audio and video recorders both share takes through the iPhone share sheet.
