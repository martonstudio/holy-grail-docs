6 TB-303

A monophonic bass line voice built on Open303.

Play it from the Digitakt on the part's channel. A loud note is an accent. A note that overlaps the previous one is a slide. Double tap a knob to reset it. Long press a knob to assign a CC (defaults CC 70 to 77).
