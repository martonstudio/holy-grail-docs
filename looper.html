6 Looper

The looper is on channel 13. It records the finished mix, effects included, and plays the loop back last. It follows the Digitakt's clock and transport.

Make a loop

  1. Run the Digitakt. The looper keeps the latest complete bars. BARS sets the length.
  2. When the box shows LOOP READY, move DIAL 1 (LOOP, default CC 70) above zero. The loop locks and plays.
  3. Move DIAL 1 back to zero to clear it and start a fresh capture.

DIAL 2 is the level of what you play live. Stop fades the loop out. There is no overdub.
