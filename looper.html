6 Looper

The looper works like the Octatrack's. It records all the time. Turn up LOOP and it locks in the last loop.

It lives on MIDI channel 13. Turn up LOOP (CC 70) and the last loop plays. LOOP is also its volume. Turn it back down to zero and the looper goes back to recording on its own.

A C note on channel 13 resets the bar, so the loop starts where you want. A pattern change does the same.

The loop has a delay, a DJ-style EQ and filter, and a divider. Use it to hold the old pattern while you bring in the next one, then fade it out.
