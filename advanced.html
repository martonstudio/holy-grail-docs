8 Advanced

SETUP on the setup page opens the audio settings.

Routing

B, parallel (default). The Digitakt plays as normal. The app adds everything on top. If the phone disconnects, runs out of battery or the app crashes, the Digitakt keeps playing.

A, round trip. Everything goes through the phone.

Bluetooth

Away from the studio? Set OUTPUT to PHONE and play into Bluetooth headphones.
