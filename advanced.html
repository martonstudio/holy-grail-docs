8 Advanced settings

Routing

The Digitakt sends one stereo mix to the app. It never sends single tracks.

RoutingWhat happens
AThe Digitakt goes through the phone with the synths, then FX, looper and master. It returns to the Digitakt MAIN over USB. If the app fails, the Digitakt is silent until you turn INT TO MAIN back on.
BThe Digitakt plays dry at its own MAIN. Its USB audio reaches the phone for loop capture only. The phone returns synths, FX and loop. App FX never reach the dry Digitakt. B is the default. If the phone is disconnected, runs out of battery or the app crashes, the Digitakt keeps playing.

Tap SETUP on the setup page. On the AUDIO page, pick ROUTING A or B.

The app uses the Digitakt's USB audio as its input. MIC is off by default. With MIC off, any input that is not the Digitakt's USB audio reaches nothing: not the output, the looper or a recording.

Bluetooth and phone output

  1. Open the AUDIO settings (tap SETUP on the setup page).
  2. Go to the TRACKS page with NEXT.
  3. Pick OUTPUT DIGITAKT or PHONE. The choice is remembered.

DIGITAKT: the app plays into the Digitakt when it is connected. Plug the Digitakt back in and it plays again. Nothing to reset.

PHONE: Digitakt audio in, output on Bluetooth headphones. The OUTPUT PHONE screen runs these steps:

  1. AUDIO STOPPED.
  2. Tap PICK HEADPHONES and choose your headphones in the system picker.
  3. AUDIO STARTED. The screen shows the route you actually got.

CANCEL returns to DIGITAKT. The steps also run at launch. At launch the picker is skipped if the output is already Bluetooth.

Changing the route during a performance does not run these steps. The app plays into whatever iOS gives.

Off the Digitakt, the output carries the Digitakt at its RECORD DT level. In routing A you hear it once. A route change during a recording closes the recording.

MIDI and AUDIO pages

Tap SETUP on the setup page. The AUDIO sheet has four pages. Turn them with PREV and NEXT.

Page 1: AUDIO

  • BUFFER: 64, 128, 256 or 512 frames. Default 128. [TODO: when to change it]
  • ROUTING: A or B. Default B. See above.
  • MIC: OFF or ON. Default OFF.
  • OTHER APPS: SILENCE or MIX. [TODO: what it does]
  • INPUT: RAW or PROCESSED. [TODO: what it does]
  • INPUT GAIN: in dB, in half-dB steps.

Page 2: AUDIO status

RUNNING (with sample rate and buffer), the Digitakt setting for the chosen routing, INPUT, OUTPUT, FORMAT, LATENCY, INPUT PEAK. An audio start error shows here.

Page 3: DIGITAKT TRACKS

  • PATTERN: the channel whose Program Change selects a pattern. Default 16. Match it to the Digitakt's PROG CHG OUT channel.
  • T1 to T6: the Digitakt's track channels. The app sends CC 95 on them in routing B. Default 1 to 6.

Page 4: TRACKS

  • T7 and T8: the Digitakt's track channels. Default 7 and 8.
  • DEMO SONG: STOP or PLAY. Plays a built-in four-part demo. [TODO: what the demo song is for]
  • OUTPUT: DIGITAKT or PHONE. See above.

RECORD DT is on the recorder page. Routing: see above.

SEND LOG, at the top of the setup page, sends the app's log.
