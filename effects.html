Effects

Audio order: the send effects (SPACE, DRIVE), then the insert (HEAT), then the looper. Effect settings are saved in the pattern. Effects have no presets.

SPACE

FX 6, MIDI channel 14. Delay, then reverb. A send effect: each part has a delay send and a reverb send.

SPACE opens on its SENDS page. SETTINGS is the second tab.

  1. Tap the SPACE box on the display grid.
  2. On SENDS, set the DELAY SEND FROM EACH PART dials and the REVERB SEND FROM EACH PART dials (CH 9 to CH 12).
  3. Tap SETTINGS for the effect itself.

DELAY: FDBK, TONE, CHAR (CLEAN, TAPE, BBD), MODE (SIMPLE, STEREO, PING PONG, MULTI TAP), TIME and TIME R, ON.

REVERB: SIZE, DECAY, DAMP, PRE, MOD, ON.

TAP sets where the send is taken: PRE FADER or POST FADER. SPACE is POST FADER by default.

SERIAL off (default): delay and reverb run side by side. Each hears only its own send. SERIAL on: the delay output also feeds the reverb.

DRIVE

FX 7, MIDI channel 15. Drive and fuzz. A send effect with the same pages and tabs as SPACE: SENDS and SETTINGS.

SENDS: DRIVE SEND FROM EACH PART and FUZZ SEND FROM EACH PART.

DRIVE: DRIVE, TONE, LEVEL, CIRCUIT (TUBE), ON.

FUZZ: DRIVE, TONE, LEVEL, CIRCUIT (FUZZ, OCTAVE, BITS, TUBE), ON.

TAP is PRE FADER by default. SERIAL feeds the drive output into the fuzz.

HEAT

FX 8, MIDI channel 16. An insert effect. It processes the parts and the send returns together.

  • DJ EQ AND FILTER: HIGH, MID, LOW, FILTER.
  • DRIVE: DRIVE, TONE, CHARACTER, CIRCUIT (TUBE, TAPE, TRANSFORMER, CLEAN BOOST), GAIN, ON.
  • BASS TO MONO: WIDTH, SLOPE, X-OVER, ON. It sums left and right below the crossover.

The FILTER is the one master filter.

Default CCs are on the Basics page.
