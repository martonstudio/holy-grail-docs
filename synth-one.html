6 Synth One

Synth One is the original AudioKit Synth One engine. Its editor has five pages: MAIN, ENV, FX, SEQ and PAD.

Play

  1. Tap a part set to SYNTH ONE on the display grid.
  2. Play notes from the Digitakt on the part's channel.
  3. Turn pages with PREV and NEXT.

Controls

MAIN: OSC 1, OSC 2 (SEMITONES, DETUNE), FILTER (FREQ, REZ, GLIDE), SUB, VOLUME, MIX, FM, NOISE, GLOBAL (WIDEN, ARP, BPM), MONO, LEGATO, ANTI-ALIASING.

ENV: FILTER ENVELOPE and AMPLITUDE ENVELOPE (ATTACK, DECAY, SUSTAIN, RELEASE).

FX: LFO 1, LFO 2, EFFECTS RACK (REVERB, DELAY), AUTOPAN, PHASER.

SEQ: arpeggiator and sequencer.

PAD: touch pad. LFO 1 RATE, FILTER RESONANCE, FILTER CUTOFF, SNAP.

CCs
