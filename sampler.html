6 Sampler

One sampler with four machines: KIT, GRAIN, STRETCH and SLICE. Tap the machine name at the top to switch.

KIT is 16 pads. GRAIN is a granular synth. SLICE is a slicer: move the slices around, and each one plays on its own MIDI note. The filter runs either all the time or from its envelope.

Load samples from your iPhone files, from your recordings, or straight from the Digitakt or Digitakt II.
