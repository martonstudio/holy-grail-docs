6 Sampler

One sampler with four machines: KIT, GRAIN, STRETCH and SLICE. The machine name sits in the middle of the header with a small arrow. Tap it to open the machine picker and switch. Switching machine asks SURE? and replaces the current kit or sample. The page title is at the left.

KIT is 16 pads. GRAIN is a granular synth with three modes: GRANULAR, SINGLE CYCLE and LONG SAMPLE. Its PERF page has a box of six dials at the right: VOLUME, ATTACK, DECAY, BITS, RATE and DRIVE. STRETCH plays a loop in time with your Digitakt. SLICE is a slicer: move the slices around, and each one plays on its own MIDI note. The filter has an envelope, and ENV RESET on the FILTER page restarts it on every note.

Load samples from your iPhone files, from your recordings, or straight from the Digitakt or Digitakt II. See Samples.
