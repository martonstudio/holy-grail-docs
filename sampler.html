6 Sampler

One engine with four machines: KIT (16 pads), GRAIN, STRETCH and SLICE. Notes from the Digitakt play it.

Choose a machine

Tap the machine name at the top left of the editor, pick a machine and confirm SURE?. Switching discards the loaded sample.

Load samples

The SAMPLE page, or an empty KIT pad, opens the sample browser. Pick a source tab, tap a file and press LOAD.

  • PHONE: the app's samples. + IMPORT copies a file from iPhone Files.
  • RECORDINGS: the audio recorder's takes.
  • DIGITAKT: pull a sample from the Digitakt over USB while its transport is stopped.

Long press a control to assign a CC. COLOR on the AMP page gives the sample a lo-fi character.
