3 Synths and CC assignment

Open a synth page

On the main page, tap a part box to open its synth page. Turn pages with PREV and NEXT. Press RETURN to go back.

Knobs and CCs

This is the most important part.

Each synth page has eight CC slots. A knob in a slot is moved by a CC from the Digitakt.

By default the slots are CC 70 to 77. These are the eight encoders on the Digitakt's MIDI track. Turn an encoder and its knob in the app moves.

Long press a knob to put it in a slot. A CC tag appears on it.

Long press it again to take it out.

If all eight slots are full, the app says so and changes nothing.

Your slots stay when you load a sound. Changing the part's engine resets them to that engine's defaults.

Change a part's engine

  1. Go to the setup page.
  2. Tap the part box.
  3. On the first page, pick an ENGINE.

Engines:

  • Synth One
  • TB-303
  • FM
  • Sampler

The setup page

The setup page shows the eight boxes: parts 1 to 4 (CH 9 to CH 12) and FX 5 to FX 8 (LOOPER, SPACE, DRIVE, HEAT). Each box shows its channel, its engine or effect and its CC numbers. The top strip has SETUP, SEND LOG and PROJECT.

  1. Tap a box.
  2. On its first page, set CHANNEL (1 to 16). On a part, set ENGINE here too.
  3. Press NEXT. Each slot has its own page: PART 1 SLOT 1 to SLOT 8, or FX n SLOT 1 to SLOT 8.

A slot page:

  • CONTROL: the control it moves.
  • ENABLED: tap to switch the row on or off. Patterns keep each row's on and off.
  • CC NUMBER: the number it answers to.
  • RANGE: drag the ends to limit what the CC covers.
  • CLEAR SLOT: removes the control.

A slot with no control says to long press a control in the editor to attach it.

A row cannot be enabled without a control. Two rows on one part cannot share a CC number.

On the CH 16 box (HEAT), the slot pages also have MIXER LEVEL. It assigns a CC to a mixer level: PART 1 to 4, DIGITAKT or MASTER.
