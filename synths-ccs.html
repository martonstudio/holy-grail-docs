3 Synths and CCs

Tap a part on the main page to open its synth page. PREV and NEXT turn pages. BACK, top left, goes back.

CC assignment

Each synth page has eight CC slots, CC 70 to 77 by default: the eight encoders of the Digitakt's MIDI track.

  • Long press a knob to put it in a slot. A box with its CC tag (for example CC 71) appears round it.
  • Long press it again to take it out.
  • If all eight slots are full, the app says so and changes nothing.

Your slots stay when you load a sound. Changing a part's engine resets them to that engine's defaults.

The setup page

The setup page shows the eight boxes with their channel, engine and CC numbers. Tap a box to set its CHANNEL, and on a part its ENGINE (Synth One, TB-303, FM or Sampler). CONTROLLERS opens the slot pages, where you set each slot's CC number, RANGE and ENABLED, or CLEAR SLOT.

On the HEAT box a slot can also drive a MIXER LEVEL.
